Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Jopply uses, why, and the choices you have.

Where your data lives

Jopply runs on your own computer. Your profile, resume, applications, notes and anything read from your email are stored in a database on that computer. Jopply's developer does not receive or store a copy.

Information Jopply uses

  • Information you provide: your resume, profile details, job preferences and answers to application questions.
  • Job postings: public listings from company career sites.
  • Gmail (optional, read-only): if you connect Gmail, Jopply searches for messages from companies you applied to and from hiring systems, and reads their sender, subject, date and text.

How Gmail data is used

Jopply uses the Gmail read-only scope (gmail.readonly) only to:

  • match replies to your applications and update their status (for example, interview or rejection);
  • show you those messages in Jopply so you can confirm or correct the status;
  • read a security code sent to you by a job application site, when you turn that option on, to finish an application you approved.

Jopply never sends, deletes, labels or modifies your email. Gmail data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build or train generalized AI models.

AI processing

To score jobs, tailor materials and recognize replies it can't classify on its own, Jopply may send the relevant text (for example a job description, your resume, or an email about an application) to Anthropic's Claude AI service, using your own account. This is done only to provide these features to you. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jopply's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Jopply shares your information only to do what you ask: application materials go to the employer's application site when you approve a submission, and text goes to the AI service as described above. It is not shared with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time in Jopply (Inbox → Disconnect), which also revokes its access, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data by deleting Jopply's data folder on your computer.

Security

Gmail access tokens and site passwords are kept on your computer; passwords are stored in the operating system's credential store.
