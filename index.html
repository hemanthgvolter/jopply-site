Your job search, on autopilot, with you in charge

Jopply is a job application assistant. It searches company career sites for roles that match your profile, prepares a tailored resume, cover letter and answers for each one, and submits only the applications you approve. It then keeps every application's status up to date in one place.

How it works

  • Find: checks the public job boards of the companies you follow for roles matching your titles and locations.
  • Prepare: scores each job against your resume and drafts the materials. Nothing is invented: it only uses your real experience.
  • Approve: you review every application before it's sent.
  • Track: with your permission, reads replies in Gmail (read-only) so each application's status updates itself: interview, rejection, offer.
  • Learn: every correction you make is remembered, so Jopply needs less of your time over time.

Gmail access

Jopply asks for read-only Gmail access only to recognize replies about your job applications. It cannot send, delete or change your mail. See the privacy policy for details.
